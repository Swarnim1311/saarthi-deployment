# Agriculture Policy Categories

The SAARTHI policy-alert feature can monitor the following categories:

1. Government agriculture schemes
2. Farmer subsidies
3. PM-KISAN updates
4. Crop insurance and PMFBY
5. Kisan Credit Card and agricultural loans
6. Minimum Support Price and procurement
7. Irrigation and water-management schemes
8. Soil-health and fertilizer-related schemes
9. Crop diversification programmes
10. State-specific schemes for Punjab farmers

## Important Information to Collect

For every policy or announcement, record:

- Policy or scheme name
- Government department
- Announcement date
- Applicable state or district
- Eligible farmers
- Crop or activity covered
- Main benefit
- Application deadline
- Required documents
- Official source link
- Verification status

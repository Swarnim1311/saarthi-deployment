agriculture-related schemes, subsidies, announcements, deadlines, and farmer
welfare updates.

## Verification Rules

Before sending any policy alert:

1. Confirm that the information comes from an official source.
2. Check the announcement date and validity period.
3. Identify the applicable state, district, or farmer group.
4. Record eligibility conditions and important deadlines.
5. Include the official source link in the alert.
6. Obtain administrator approval before sending the message.
7. Provide an opt-out option for farmers.

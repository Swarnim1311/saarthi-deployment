# Agriculture Policy Update Workflow

The SAARTHI policy-alert feature should follow this workflow:

## 1. Collect Information

Monitor verified government websites for new agriculture-related schemes,
subsidies, announcements, deadlines, and farmer welfare updates.

## 2. Identify New Updates

Check whether the announcement is new and whether it has already been
recorded in the system.

## 3. Verify the Information

Confirm the following details:

- Official government source
- Announcement date
- Applicable state, district, or block
- Eligible farmer groups
- Main benefit
- Application deadline
- Required documents
- Official application link

## 4. Prepare a Farmer-Friendly Summary

Convert the official announcement into simple language. The summary should
clearly explain:

- What is the scheme or update?
- Who can benefit?
- What is the main benefit?
- How can farmers apply?
- What is the deadline?
- Where can farmers get more information?

## 5. Administrator Approval

An authorized administrator should review and approve the message before it
is sent to farmers.

## 6. Send Notifications

After approval, send the update through suitable channels such as:

- SMS
- WhatsApp
- SAARTHI website
- Mobile application
- Agriculture officer or extension-worker dashboard

## 7. Track Delivery

Record whether the notification was:

- Sent
- Delivered
- Failed
- Read, where supported

## 8. Maintain an Opt-Out Option

Farmers should be able to subscribe to or unsubscribe from policy alerts at
any time.

## Basic Flow

Official Government Source  
→ Information Collection  
→ Duplicate Checking  
→ Verification  
→ Farmer-Friendly Summary  
→ Administrator Approval  
→ SMS/WhatsApp Notification  
→ Delivery Tracking
